Privacy, in plain language.

Your family archive stays on your computer. Photo Exit Manifest is designed not to need it.

CLI data

The CLI reads the folders you explicitly name and writes reports only to the output path you choose. It does not upload photos, filenames, hashes, metadata, policies, or manifests. It has no analytics, telemetry, account system, or network calls.

Website data

The website does not inspect local folders and uses no advertising or behavioral analytics. The readiness planner operates in browser memory. If you paste or receive a purchase license, the token and its most recent verification result are stored in your browser’s local storage.

Purchase verification

License verification sends only the license token to the Sociobot billing API. Checkout and refunds are handled by Sociobot / Dodo as merchant of record under their policies. We do not receive your photo library.

Your choices

Remove the saved license at any time by clearing this site’s local storage. Uninstalling the CLI and deleting its output reports removes all product-created local data. Reports may contain filenames or device-owner names, so store them with the same care as your archive.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s public source repository.
